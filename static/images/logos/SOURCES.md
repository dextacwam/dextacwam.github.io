# Institution logo sources

- `uiuc.png`: University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign formal wordmark, downloaded unchanged from https://brand.illinois.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Illinois_formal_wordmark_fullcolor_rgb-1-1.png . Brand reference: https://brand.illinois.edu/visual-identity/wordmarks .
- `uc-berkeley.png`: UC Berkeley wordmark, rasterized to 1125x210 from the official vector wordmark served by Berkeley's brand portal at https://brand.berkeley.edu/wp-content/themes/gallantry/dist/imgs/logo-uc-berkeley.svg . The source SVG is the white-on-dark variant used in the brand portal header; the fill was recolored to the official Berkeley Blue (#003262) for use on a light background. Geometry and aspect ratio are unchanged. Brand reference: https://brand.berkeley.edu/visual-identity/logos/ .

- `northwestern.png`: Northwestern University wordmark, rasterized to 1000x134 from the official vector wordmark at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Northwestern_University_wordmark.svg , which credits Northwestern's own brand assets page (http://www.northwestern.edu/brand/brand-assets/#wordmark) as its source and is in the public domain. The file already uses the official Northwestern Purple (#4E2A84), so no recoloring was applied. This is the "Northwestern" wordmark, which the brand site designates as the preferred version ("the word University is not needed" in most cases). Brand reference: https://www.northwestern.edu/brand/visual-identity/logos-lockups/northwestern-wordmark-lockup/ .

The marks retain their original geometry and aspect ratios. They identify the authors' university affiliations.

Note: Berkeley's downloadable logo package (brand essentials toolkit) is gated behind CalNet
authentication, so the portal's own published vector wordmark was used instead.

Note: Northwestern's brand site links its wordmark files through a gated digital asset manager,
so the publicly mirrored vector of the same mark was used instead.
